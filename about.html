About ClothPedia

A story of calm, culture, and curiosity — lovingly crafted by Haseeb & Shehzam.

Why We Built This

ClothPedia is our creative response to a noisy world — a place where fashion is not rushed, but reflected on. We believe every fabric has a story, every season a mood, and every culture a thread to weave in.

We started this as an academic project, but it became a passion. Through visuals, design, and words, we hope to bring you closer to what you wear — and why it matters.

It’s more than a catalog. It’s a calm encyclopedia of clothing, made with care.
